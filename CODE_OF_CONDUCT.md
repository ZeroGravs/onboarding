# Code of conduct

## Our expectations

Help make Onboarding Kit a welcoming, respectful place to collaborate, regardless
of background, identity, disability, experience, or level of familiarity with the
project.

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; do not attack people.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and differences of opinion.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and give others room to learn.
- Keep participation relevant and avoid unsolicited promotion or spam.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, impersonation, and sharing
someone's private information without permission are not acceptable. Neither are
retaliation against a person who raises a concern or repeated disruptive behavior.

## Scope and moderation

This policy applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
official project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, issue warnings, or
restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Decisions should consider
context, severity, repeated behavior, and the safety of participants. Maintainers
are expected to follow the same standards.

## Raising a concern

Do not publish personal information, private correspondence, or sensitive details
in a public issue.

For abuse on GitHub, use the relevant content or profile's **Report abuse** action,
or [GitHub's private abuse-reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
These reports reach GitHub and are handled under its platform policies; they do
not create a private report to this project's maintainers.

Until a dedicated project conduct contact is published, ask maintainers for a
private channel without posting sensitive details if you need project-specific
mediation. Do not use the vulnerability-reporting channel for non-security
conduct concerns.

Report software vulnerabilities according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
